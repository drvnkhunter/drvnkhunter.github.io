About Me

I am a Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Professional with +3 years of experience spanning IT operations, DevOps, and Application Security. Currently, I serve as an HPE NonStop Tandem Consultant, ensuring that high-availability, fault-tolerant financial architectures remain highly secure, resilient, and robust. Furthermore, I have ongoing experience creating secure frameworks for mobile applications and conducting comprehensive vulnerability analyses (SAST/DAST) using tools such as Veracode, MobSF, and Snyk Code.
